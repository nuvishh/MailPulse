🤖 How to get a Telegram bot

  1. Open Telegram and chat with @BotFather → /newbot → follow the steps.
  2. Copy the bot token it gives you and paste it above.
  3. Start a chat with your new bot (tap Start).
  4. Open https://api.telegram.org/bot<token>/getUpdates in your browser, find "chat":{"id":...} — that's your chat ID.
  5. Paste it above, save, and hit Send test message.
