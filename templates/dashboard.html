How to use with Gmail

  1. Click + New tracked email and enter the recipient & subject. Optionally add trackable links and a follow-up reminder.
  2. Copy the pixel image URL shown on the next page.
  3. In Gmail compose, click the Insert photo (🖼️) icon → Web address (URL) → paste the URL → Insert. Set the image size to Small.
  4. For tracked links: paste the tracking link URL (the /c/… one) as the hyperlink in your mail instead of the direct URL.
  5. Send your mail as usual. Enable Telegram in ⚙️ Settings for instant open/click notifications.

Note: if the recipient's mail app blocks images, the open won't register. Tracking is reliable in most cases but not 100%.
